**LSU 51, Clemson 10.** Lane Kiffin's first game in Baton Rouge started two hours late because of lightning and ended about ten minutes after that. 31-3 at the half, 336 yards to 54, and Jordan Ross took an interception to the house before Clemson had crossed midfield. Sam Leavitt threw for 230 and ran for 113 like a man who'd heard the "game manager" talk and took it personally. Dilin Jones added 113 more on the ground, Winston Watkins Jr. caught seven for 110, and Clemson's Christopher Vizzina finished with 83 passing yards and got pulled in the second quarter. Clemson's worst loss in thirty years, and Dabo's review was "the best thing about that game is it's over." Coach, same.

**Michigan 13, Western Michigan 12.** Michigan was a 27.5-point favorite and won on a Hail Mary that happened after the game was over. Bryce Underwood's first one sailed out of bounds, the clock said zero, Western Michigan's sideline started living its best life, and then replay found one second. One. Second. Underwood hit JJ Buchanan for 47 yards on the mulligan and Kyle Whittingham's first win at Michigan is going to be a bar trivia question until the sun burns out. Western Michigan out-rushed them, forced three turnovers, and had a kicker go four for four from 52, 45, 39 and 33. The Broncos did everything except own a watch.

**Oregon 34, Boise State 27.** The No. 2 team in the country, at home, giving 24.5, and tied 27-27 with eight minutes left. Dante Moore was excellent (28 of 37, 378 yards, three scores) and Oregon still needed a 62-yard bomb to Dakorien Moore with 7:51 left to breathe. Maddux Madsen threw three touchdowns and Boise State didn't turn it over once. Twenty-four and a half was a number somebody made up. Boise is a real program with a real quarterback, and if you call them a mid-major again I'm assuming you didn't watch.

**For the record.** No picks posted this week, so no receipts, but I'll say it anyway. I'd have taken Boise plus the points and Michigan minus the 27.5. One for two, and the wrong one. That's the standard here. Wins and losses stay up.

Somewhere in South Carolina, Dabo is telling a room full of boosters the schedule was hard. The schedule was Tiger Stadium. In September. Against a team that lost its coach in November.
