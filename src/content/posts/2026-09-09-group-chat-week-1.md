"Nothing changes" is what you say when everything changed.

The Vikings spent the 10th overall pick on J.J. McCarthy in 2024, then signed Kyler Murray to a one-year deal in March and handed him the job two weeks into camp. Kevin O'Connell said all the right things about McCarthy's "arrow up." Coaches don't say "arrow up" about starters. They say it about players they're trying to keep in the building.

Watch the sideline body language Sunday at home against Green Bay. It will tell you more than the box score.
