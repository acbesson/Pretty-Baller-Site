Preseason heat index, updated before a single snap counts.

The ranking is simple. It's not the record. It's the distance between what the coach says in the press conference and what the roster says on the field. Aaron Glenn's Jets went 3-14 and responded by hiring a new coordinator, which is what you do when you're out of ideas and out of time. Todd Bowles lost his two best veterans and his quarterback's trust in the same offseason. Zac Taylor has the best quarterback on this list and the worst results. Shane Steichen paid the quarterback who got hurt and is now praying he doesn't get hurt again.

Readings update every Monday. Coaches, please don't make this easy.
